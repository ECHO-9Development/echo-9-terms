ECHO-9 AI — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ECHO-9 Discord bot and its local alert bridge handle information. It applies to the bot and the related ECHO-9 Discord integration operated by its owner.

1. Information We Process

When you use the bot, Discord provides information needed to handle interactions. This can include your Discord user ID, the server (guild) and channel IDs, command names, interaction permissions, and the text you enter into a command.

When ECHO-9 or an authorized local integration sends an alert, the bridge processes the alert title, details, and severity. Alert text may contain information about your computer or ECHO-9 environment, depending on what the sender includes.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To respond to bot commands such as status, help, and authorized test-alert commands.
  • To deliver security and system notifications to the Discord channel configured by the operator.
  • To diagnose errors, maintain the integration, and protect the service from unauthorized use.

3. Storage and Retention

The current bot implementation does not include a database or a feature that intentionally saves a user profile or command history. It writes operational messages and errors to the process console/log output. Alert messages sent to Discord may remain in Discord according to the server's settings and Discord's retention practices. The operator may separately retain local system logs or alert records outside the bot; their retention depends on the device and configuration.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

The bot does not sell personal information. Information entered into Discord is handled by Discord under its own policies. Alert content is sent to the Discord channel configured by the operator and is visible to people who have permission to view that channel. Do not include information in alerts that channel members should not see.

5. Security

The local alert bridge is configured to listen on the local computer (127.0.0.1) and requires a secret bearer token. This is a security measure, not a guarantee that every device, account, network, or integration is secure. Keep bot tokens and bridge secrets private. Never send passwords, authentication codes, private keys, payment information, or other highly sensitive data through bot commands or alerts.

6. Your Choices

You can stop using the bot and avoid submitting additional information. You can ask the ECHO-9 operator about information contained in locally maintained logs or alert records. Messages already posted to Discord may need to be handled through Discord or by a server moderator; the bot does not provide an automated data-export or deletion command.

7. Children

The bot is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use it to submit a child's personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the ECHO-9 administrator through the official ECHO-9 support server. The operator should provide a current contact method in that server.
